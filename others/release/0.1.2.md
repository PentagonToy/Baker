# Baker 0.1.2

This update improves Scan reliability, capture cleanup and formula output.

- Cancel Scan during setup or processing without losing captured pages. Prevent overlapping window-selection work.
- Turn pages with Right Arrow, Down Arrow or a selected button.
- Click **X** on a capture thumbnail to move only that capture to Trash. Saving, dragging and automatic dismissal keep it.
- Keep separate formula rows and annotations in previews and Keynote output, with clearer controls and a refreshed Workspace icon.
- Improve macOS 26 build compatibility and add automated build and core-test checks.

The app and DMG are Developer ID signed and notarised by Apple. Existing captures, models and settings are preserved.
