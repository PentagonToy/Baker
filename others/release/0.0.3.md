# Baker 0.0.3

**A keen eye. A fresh result.**

This update refines capture editing, recording, history and settings, and adds optional update checks. Recognition stays local; the PDF engine and models are unchanged.

## Changes

- Remove plain or smoothly shaded paper backgrounds, or choose Photo Cutout for subjects. Trace colour or monochrome SVGs with VTracer and Potrace. Undo and Redo remain available; Done retains one PNG or SVG per capture. Inspect faint details against the original.
- Use the compact editor, Okabe–Ito palette, white masking preset and Repeat Area command.
- Record MP4 with optional system audio for up to three minutes, or silent GIF for up to 30 seconds. Browse image, formula, video and GIF history through thumbnail cards and media filters.
- Manage image and formula history in General. Images have no retention limit initially; cache clearing and history refresh remove records whose files are already missing.
- Select up to five of thirteen offline text OCR languages, reorder menu commands by dragging and optionally enable login startup.
- Improve Unicode primes, scripts, accents and mathematical alphabets. Unsupported structures retain their source; Unicode remains an approximation of typeset maths.
- Preserve Settings state, improve error recovery and PDF cancellation, and reduce repeated thumbnail and progress-file reads without adding a resident OCR worker.
- Show Screen Recording status with System Settings and Reopen Baker actions. Check stable app updates manually, or enable a quiet daily check; review notes and download the DMG in the browser.

## Installation

Download **Baker-0.0.3-arm64.dmg**, open it and drag Baker to Applications. Apple Silicon and **macOS 26.0 or later** are required; **PDF conversion requires macOS 26.2 or later**. The optional PDF Engine 1.0.0 downloads about 1.06 GB once, then works offline. Capture text, formulas and previews work offline with the included runtime.

The app is ad-hoc signed and **not notarised**. macOS may block a downloaded copy or request Screen Recording permission again after replacement. See [first-launch and permission guidance](https://pentagontoy.github.io/Baker/help/troubleshooting.html). Existing captures and preferences remain outside the app bundle.
