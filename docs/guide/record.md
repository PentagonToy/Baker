# Record

Choose **Capture → Record…**, select an area, then choose **Video** or **GIF** in the floating panel. Check the output dimensions, choose whether to show the cursor and press **Record**. The region button selects a different area; Escape or the close button cancels setup. The menu remembers the last started format. Existing Video and GIF shortcuts open the same panel with their respective format selected.

Completed recordings are saved automatically in the chosen save folder and appear in [Capture History](recent.md). **Save…** in the Export panel creates another copy; **Save & Copy…** also copies that file for pasting. If automatic saving fails, Baker keeps the temporary recording available for manual saving and reports the failure.

Video offers a **System audio** checkbox for sounds from apps and media; Baker does not record the microphone. GIF always has no sound. Protected content may prevent audio or video capture.

During recording, the compact panel shows elapsed time, the duration limit, Stop and Discard. Discard removes the recording; it does not save a partial file. Setup and recording controls are excluded from the recording, while ordinary Baker workspaces can remain visible. Trimming and pause/resume are not available. See [limits](../help/limits.md) for more detail.
