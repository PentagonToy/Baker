# Record

Choose **Video…** or **GIF…**, select an area, then record. Use **Stop** to finish or **Cancel** to discard.

| Format | Duration | Frame rate | Longest side |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../assets/symbols/record-video-dark.svg"><img src="../../others/assests/symbols/record-video.svg" width="22" alt="Video"></picture> Silent MP4 | 60 seconds | Up to 30 fps | 1920px |
| <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../assets/symbols/record-gif-dark.svg"><img src="../../others/assests/symbols/record-gif.svg" width="22" alt="GIF"></picture> Looping GIF | 15 seconds | Up to 10 fps | 960px |

Choose **Save…** in the Export panel to keep the file. **Save & Copy…** also copies the saved file for pasting. Save before starting another export or quitting.

Recording excludes Baker’s own windows. Audio and trimming are not available. See [limits](../help/limits.md) for more detail.
